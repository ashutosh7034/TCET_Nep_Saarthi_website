About the NEP SAARTHI Initiative

What is NEP SAARTHI?

The NEP SAARTHI (Student Ambassador for Academic Reforms in Transforming Higher Education in India) initiative by the University Grants Commission (UGC) aims to empower students to become change-makers in the implementation of the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020.

SAARTHIs are student representatives who promote awareness, gather feedback, and assist in implementing NEP-driven reforms across higher education institutions. Our institution took its first step toward this reformative journey in January 2025, nominating nine dedicated student ambassadors.

Our Vision & Mission

Vision

NEP SAARTHI envisions creating a student-driven movement that actively contributes to shaping a future-ready, inclusive, and multidisciplinary education ecosystem as envisioned by the National Education Policy 2020. We aspire to build a generation of informed, empowered, and responsible learners who are not just beneficiaries but also co-creators of India's evolving academic landscape.

Mission

  • Promote Awareness: Educate students, faculty, and the wider community about the key tenets of NEP 2020 through dynamic campaigns.
  • Encourage Participation: Foster a culture of dialog where students contribute directly to the implementation of academic reforms.
  • Bridge the Gap: Serve as an active link between students, educators, and policymakers to align ground-level expectations.
  • Empower through Leadership: Nurture student leaders who champion digital transformation, flexibility, and skill development.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Academic Bank of Credits (ABC)?

The Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) is a digital repository established by the UGC to store academic credits earned by students. It acts like a commercial bank database, storing credits gained from recognized institutions, which can then be retrieved and transferred for degree validation across India.

How does the Multiple Entry & Exit option benefit students?

Under NEP 2020, students are no longer locked into rigid 4-year timelines. They can exit after 1 year with an undergraduate Certificate, after 2 years with a Diploma, after 3 years with a Bachelor's Degree, or complete the full 4 years with a Bachelor's Degree with Research. Credits are stored in ABC, allowing re-entry to finish education later.

What is the role of an NEP SAARTHI?

An NEP SAARTHI (Student Ambassador) represents the UGC-backed leadership network on campus. Their core role is to bridge the communication gap between university authorities and students, organizing awareness camps, addressing policy queries, and facilitating credit systems like ABC.

How do I create and link my ABC account?

You can create your ABC ID via DigiLocker. Go to the DigiLocker app or website, register using your Aadhaar details, select Academic Bank of Credits under education services, enter your university details, and copy your 12-digit ABC ID to register it with the TCET academic portal.
