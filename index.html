About the NEP SAARTHI Initiative

What is the Role?

Launched by the University Grants Commission (UGC), the Student Ambassador for Academic Reforms in Transforming Higher Education (NEP SAARTHI) initiative appoints student leaders as primary catalysts to drive awareness and gather qualitative student feedback regarding UGC reforms.

TCET took its inaugural step by nominating and enrolling nine dedicated student ambassadors in January 2025. This cohort acts as a crucial communication bridge, fostering academic transparency and encouraging peers to utilize regional credit nodes.

Promoting Policy Awareness

Conducting peer seminars, department briefings, and dynamic campaigns to clarify key features of the NEP 2020 framework.

Gathering Student Feedback

Representing students in academic council discussions and conveying peer concerns to authorities to refine reform implementation.

Digital Systems Assistance

Hosting hands-on camps to guide student registration for the Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) through DigiLocker.

Educational Horizon

Our Core Vision

We envision building a student-led educational movement that transforms standard learning pathways into inclusive, multidisciplinary, and credit-mobile frameworks. We seek to cultivate active student participation as co-creators of India's higher education system, bridging policies with campus realities.

The NEP Reform Timeline

July 2020

National Education Policy 2020 Approved

The Union Cabinet of India approves the new framework, replacing the 1986 policy. Envisions critical changes toward credit mobility, flexible exits, and interdisciplinary study.

July 2021

Establishment of Academic Bank of Credits (ABC)

The UGC formally introduces ABC system regulations, enabling universities to digitally record, verify, and store academic credits earned by students.

February 2022

DigiLocker Marksheet Integration

UGC coordinates with DigiLocker to ensure digital verification of marks and certificates, granting them equal legal status as physical copies.

December 2023

Choice-Based Curriculum Upgrades

Implementation of Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) updates across HEIs, allowing cross-disciplinary major and minor structures.

June 2024

Vocational Credit Framework Released

Launch of unified National Credit Framework (NCrF) guidelines linking academic, vocational, and skill-based learning into a single index.

January 2025

TCET Appoints NEP SAARTHI Cohort

Appointment of 9 student ambassadors at TCET Kandivali. Launch of Shiksha Saptah orientation programs, Samvaad panel series, and digital credit linking support camps.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Academic Bank of Credits (ABC)?

The Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) is a virtual credit storage system set up by the UGC under the NEP 2020. It accumulates credits earned by students from authorized institutions, allowing simple verification, storage, and mobility for transfer applications.

How do multiple entries and exits operate?

If a student leaves after Year 1, they receive a formal Undergraduate Certificate; after Year 2, an Undergraduate Diploma; after Year 3, a Bachelor’s Degree; and after Year 4, a Bachelor’s Degree with Research. Credits remain stored in their digital ABC wallet for eventual course re-entry.

What is the core role of a student SAARTHI?

Student SAARTHIs act as campus contacts who organize counseling hours, handle peer queries about CBCS and major-minor selections, and conduct registration campaigns to get students enrolled on the ABC platform.
